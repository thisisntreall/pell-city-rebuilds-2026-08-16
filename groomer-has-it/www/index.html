Diana

A special love they can actually sense.

The mission on every directory listing is still the true one: make your fur babies feel like they are at home. Diana started grooming in Pinson and has kept a Pell City salon on Martin Street South for years — between 19th / Mays and Colt Cain Road.

Neighbors mention patience, reasonable prices, nail work, and the willingness to learn a new dog. The old GoDaddy page is gone. This is the shop, stated plainly, so St. Clair County can find the phone number.

What to ask for when you call

Pricing depends on coat, condition, and temperament — Diana quotes by the dog, not a laminated menu that punishes a shepherd.

Full groom

Bath, dry, haircut or breed trim, nails, ears, sanitary. The standing appointment most regulars keep.

Bath & tidy

Wash, blowout, nails, face and feet. For the weeks between full clips.

Deshed & double coats

Undercoat work for the big Alabama-summer dogs. Bring the shepherd. Bring the doodle.

Nails & nervous first visits

Short, kind sessions. If your dog hates the table, say so when you book — she’ll plan the day around it.
